5. ANIMATION DIRECTIONS — PRESERVED USER WORDING AND REUSABLE FORM
Preserved user direction from the visible prior conversation:
"first he looks into map, confuses. now he scrathes his head while looking into
map. then looks both sides in consufion while holding both hands on map. and
then looks into map like a loop. generate the same for both light and dark.
in dark we use head light too."

Reusable normalized motion prompt (not an original tool prompt):
Animate only the reference traveler in a smooth four-second loop at 24 fps.
Start looking down at the map, puzzled. Scratch the head while continuing to
look at the map, then return that hand to the map. Holding the map in both
hands, look to one side and then the other with confusion, then return to the
initial map-reading pose. Match the ending pose to the beginning for a smooth
loop. Keep both feet planted, body scale and screen position consistent, and
camera completely locked: no zoom, pan, shake or reframing. Preserve face,
hair, hands, map, backpack and clothes; avoid morphing or flicker. No walking,
bouncing or movement of the environment. For night, preserve the headlamp and
its warm face/map illumination. Use the corresponding day/night reference.

Implementation constraint: keep the 404 artwork static. Animate clouds as a
separate web layer. Composite only the transparent character video over the
scene. Retain four-second pacing without discarding every other frame.
